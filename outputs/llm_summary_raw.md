Key Findings
Total Discoveries and Timeline: A total of 6,336 planets were recorded across a discovery year range spanning from 1992 to 2026.

Dominant Discovery Methods: Transit is the primary discovery method with 4,676 planets, followed by Radial Velocity with 1,197 planets and Microlensing with 282 planets.

Planet Size Distribution: Neptune-like planets are the most common size class (2,082), closely followed by Super-Jupiter (1,828) and Super-Earth (1,195). Earth-sized planets total 490, while Sub-Earths number 86.

Temperature Bands: Most planets fall into the Hot (2,666) or Warm (2,334) temperature bands, with 333 classified as temperate proxies and 61 identified as habitable proxies.

Distance and Recent Peaks: The 100-1,000 pc distance band contains the highest concentration of planets (3,632). Within the recent yearly breakdown, 2016 recorded the highest single-year discovery count with 1,504 planets.

Limitations of the Data
Missing Values and Unknown Classifications: The data contains missing entries, specifically 0.8% missing radius data and 0.5% missing mass data. Furthermore, certain categorical fields include "Unknown" counts (e.g., 50 planets have an unknown size class, 526 have unknown temperature bands, and 27 have unknown distance bands).

Incomplete Yearly Breakdown: The detailed yearly discovery breakdown only covers recent years (2012 to 2026), leaving yearly discovery counts for the period between 1992 and 2011 unspecified.

Lack of Combined Parameter Distributions: The computed findings present individual aggregates (like median radius and median mass) independently, but do not provide cross-tabulations or joint distributions (e.g., mass vs. radius or mass vs. distance) to evaluate multi-variable relationships.

Suggestions for Further Analysis
Cross-Tabulate Discovery Methods and Size Classes: Analyze how different discovery methods correlate with planet size and temperature classifications to identify potential observational biases.

Examine Habitable Proxies: Perform a targeted analysis of the 61 habitable proxy planets against their distance bands, discovery methods, and temperature classifications to understand their distribution.
